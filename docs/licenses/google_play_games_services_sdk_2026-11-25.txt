Google Play Games Services SDK license and integration references
Verified: 2026-11-25
Artifact: com.google.android.gms:play-services-games-v2:21.0.0

Google's Android developer setup instructions require an OAuth client for authentication and
a Play Console credential linked to the game; they describe the SDK and the OAuth consent-screen
scopes required by Play Games Services. The SDK is distributed by Google via Google Maven.

Sources:
- https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/console/setup
- https://developer.android.com/studio/terms
- https://developers.google.com/terms
- https://policies.google.com/privacy
