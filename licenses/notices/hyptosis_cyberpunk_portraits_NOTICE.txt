Cyberpunk Portraits
https://opengameart.org/content/cyberpunk-portraits

Author: Hyptosis (https://opengameart.org/users/hyptosis)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). The page says the art can be used for anything.

Used in Atlantis Ascension: the five dialogue portraits (assets/art/generated/portraits/*.png) start from
faces of Cyber_Faces_001.png and are rebuilt by tools/gen_portraits.py (recolor, costume pieces drawn by code,
expressions by local warping, lighting and background). Credited in the game's credits screen even though CC0
does not require it.
